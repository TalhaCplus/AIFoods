Frequently asked questions

How AIFoods works, what it connects to, and what to expect in the first weeks.

General

What is AIFoods?

AIFoods is a set of AI agents for restaurants. They answer calls, take orders, qualify catering requests, and turn every conversation into reporting on where revenue is growing.

Who is it for?

Independent restaurants and multi-location groups in the US that get more phone and catering demand than their staff can answer during service.

Will guests know they’re talking to AI?

The agent sounds natural and uses your restaurant’s greeting and tone. If a guest asks, it says it’s an AI assistant and can transfer them to your team.

Ordering and catering

Can it handle our full menu?

Yes. It supports modifiers, combos, substitutions, and sold-out items, and updates when your menu changes.

What happens if a guest wants a person?

The call is transferred to your team along with a short summary, so the guest doesn’t repeat themselves.

How does a catering request reach my team?

Once the agent has the date, headcount, service style, dietary needs, and budget, it sends a brief to your catering lead by text and email.

Setup and systems

Does it work with our POS?

Orders are sent to your existing point-of-sale system with items, modifiers, and pickup times, so the kitchen workflow stays the same.

How long does setup take?

Most restaurants are taking live calls within two weeks. The main step is loading and checking your menu.

Do we need new hardware?

No. AIFoods runs on your existing phone number and systems.

Reporting

What does Revenue Intelligence show?

Demand by location, time of day, channel, and request type, plus conversion rates, average order value, and where inquiries stall.

Can I ask it questions?

Yes. Type a question like “Where is catering growing?” and it answers from your own data.

Works with your POS.

Orders land in the point-of-sale system your kitchen already uses, with modifiers, pickup times, and totals intact. No new tablet on the counter.
